Primary work,

A screen is not one space. It is a hierarchy: persistent chrome, primary work, secondary context, and transient overlays. Negative space is the material that keeps them apart.

Tell the truth,
then get out of the way.

Banners stay. Toasts pass. Skeletons preserve the shape of the answer. Empty states explain the next move. A destructive action asks once, clearly, with the consequence in the sentence.
